# Sagarvani --- Master Project Brief

## Project

**Sagarvani --- Conversational Marine Decision-Intelligence Platform**

-   Smart India Hackathon 2026
-   Problem Statement: **SIH26176 --- ORCA: Marine Ecosystem Reasoning
    with Collaborative Agents**
-   Theme: Disaster Management
-   Category: Software
-   Team: **Helios Luna**
-   Institution: Dayananda Sagar University
-   Repository: https://github.com/SyedAmaan001/Sagarvaani
-   Public product site currently associated with the repository:
    https://sagarvaani.vercel.app/

## Product in one sentence

Sagarvani lets a user ask a marine question in natural language and uses
ORCA to coordinate specialist agents, fuse ocean/weather/geospatial
evidence, validate contradictions, and return an explainable
recommendation.

## Core proposition

**USER QUESTION → ORCA interprets intent → specialist marine agents
collaborate → evidence is fused and contradictions checked →
validated/explainable answer**

## Primary users

1.  Fishermen & coastal crews
2.  Marine & port operators
3.  Ocean researchers & scientists
4.  Disaster management & coastal safety
5.  Aquaculture & marine farming
6.  Coastal tourism & recreation
7.  Offshore energy / marine industry
8.  Marine policy & conservation

## Access paths

-   Web / App
-   Helpline
-   Low-bandwidth Communication Portal

## MVP decisions confirmed by the team

-   Hero direction: **WaveHero + small product UI overlay**
-   Demo scenario: existing scripted scenario is acceptable
-   Voice: **in MVP**
-   Login: **none; Launch Console goes directly to dashboard**
-   Team presentation: **Team Helios Luna only**; no public individual
    team/contact information is required

## Experience principles

-   Mission-control for the ocean, not generic chat
-   Calm under pressure
-   Spatial first
-   Evidence before confidence
-   Visible validation
-   No invented statistics or customer testimonials
-   No pricing/payment section
-   Do not claim Sagarvani guarantees safety or replaces official
    authorities

## Visual direction

The public website takes structural and interaction inspiration from
COSMOQ, but Sagarvani's brand, content, data, imagery, and product
language are original.

The product UI uses the **Electric Tundra** system: - Primary
background: `#050A30` - Accent blue: `#0000FF` - Accent cyan:
`#00FFFF` - Elevated surface: `#0B1550` - Sunken/map surface:
`#02051C` - Border: `#1B2A6B` - Primary text: `#F4F7FF` - Secondary
text: `#93A2E0` - Success: `#22E29A` - Warning: `#FFB020` - Danger:
`#FF4D6D`

## Implementation philosophy

Inspect the current repository first. Preserve working dashboard
functionality. Reuse existing components and infrastructure where
possible. Build the marketing site and dashboard as one coherent system,
with a clean mock-to-real API boundary.
