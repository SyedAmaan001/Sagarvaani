# Sagarvani --- Website Specification

## Reference

Base structural inspiration: COSMOQ Framer homepage.

Preserve the reference's high-level rhythm: navigation → hero → feature
differentiation → CTA → tabbed explanation → agent/product showcase →
steps → trust/reliability → impact → FAQ → technology/data strip → final
CTA → footer.

Do not reproduce COSMOQ's brand, claims, pricing, testimonials, or
proprietary copy.

## Navigation

-   Logo → `/`
-   Solutions → `/solutions`
-   How It Works → `/how-it-works`
-   Access Channels → `/access`
-   Contact → `/contact`
-   CTA: **Try Sagarvani** → `/access` or `/dashboard`

## Hero

Announcement: **Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement
SIH26176**

Headline: **Marine Intelligence, One Conversation Away**

Subheading: Sagarvani understands your question, gathers ocean, weather,
and geospatial data, and reasons across every source to deliver a
validated, explainable recommendation --- accessible from smartphones to
simple helplines.

Primary CTA: **Try Sagarvani** Secondary CTA: **See How It Works**

### Confirmed hero direction

**WaveHero + small product UI overlay.**

The WaveHero is the main visual field. Overlay a compact Sagarvani
console fragment showing: - user question - ORCA status - agent
activity - small map - recommendation / confidence - evidence indicator

Do not make the overlay so large that it turns the hero back into a
static dashboard screenshot.

## Powered-by strip

Use real ecosystem/data/technology names where appropriate: INCOIS, IMD,
ISRO Bhuvan/NRSC, Bhoonidhi, MOSDAC, BHASHINI, Sarvam AI, plus selected
implementation technologies where useful.

## What Sets Sagarvani Apart

1.  Multi-Agent Intelligence
2.  Re-planning on Contradictions
3.  Explainable Recommendations

Supporting highlight: **One Intelligence Engine, Three Access Paths**

## All-in-one Marine Intelligence

Use tabs analogous to the reference: - Understand - Orchestrate -
Validate

Each tab explains the corresponding stage and shows a supporting visual.

## Stakeholder explorer

Priority order: 1. Fishermen & Coastal Crews 2. Marine & Port Operators
3. Ocean Researchers & Scientists 4. Disaster Management & Coastal
Safety 5. Aquaculture & Marine Farming 6. Coastal Tourism & Recreation
7. Offshore Energy / Marine Industry 8. Marine Policy & Conservation

Use benefit-focused language; do not invent outcome statistics.

## Agents

Show six: - Marine Data Agent - Weather Agent - GIS Agent - Ocean
Analytics Agent - Risk Agent - Spatial Reasoning Agent

Use DepthCarousel where the supplied implementation is intended.

## 3-step flow

1.  Ask your question
2.  ORCA orchestrates specialist agents
3.  Receive a validated, explainable recommendation

## Reliability

Show: - source cross-checking - contradiction detection - re-planning -
fallback/data-source diversity - evidence trace - confidence state

Do not imply perfect certainty.

## Our Approach

Use the supplied case-study/stack interaction rather than fake
testimonials.

## Impact

Frame social, economic and environmental dimensions without unsupported
numerical claims.

## FAQ

Required topics: - What is Sagarvani? - What is ORCA? - What data
sources are used? - Who is it for? - How does low-connectivity access
work? - What languages/voice support exists? - How are contradictions
handled? - What SIH problem/theme is it addressing?

## Technology & data

Separate: - Marine/data sources - AI/orchestration - geospatial stack -
voice/language stack - deployment/infrastructure

## Final CTA

**Step Into Sagarvani --- Marine Intelligence for Everyone**

Subtext: From open water to the coastline --- one validated answer,
however you reach us.

## Footer

Show only: **Team Helios Luna · Smart India Hackathon 2026** and problem
statement/theme information as needed.

Do not include individual team contacts/social handles. Do not include
template credits, Framer badge, or payment links.
