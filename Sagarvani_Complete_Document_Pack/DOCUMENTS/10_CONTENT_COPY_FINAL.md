# Sagarvani --- Approved Core Copy

## Hero

**Marine Intelligence, One Conversation Away**

Sagarvani understands your question, gathers ocean, weather, and
geospatial data, and reasons across every source to deliver a validated,
explainable recommendation --- accessible from smartphones to simple
helplines.

## Differentiation

### Multi-Agent Intelligence

Six specialized agents fuse ocean, weather and geospatial data instead
of leaving users to interpret fragmented information manually.

### Re-planning on Contradictions

When sources conflict or confidence is low, the reasoning and validation
layer re-checks and re-analyzes before the answer is presented.

### Explainable Recommendations

Recommendations come with evidence-backed reasoning so users can
understand why a result was produced.

### One Intelligence Engine, Three Access Paths

The same intelligence layer is designed to reach users through Web/App,
Helpline and low-bandwidth communication.

## Process

### Ask

Ask the marine question naturally.

### Orchestrate

ORCA coordinates the specialist agents and relevant data.

### Validate

Evidence is cross-checked and the final recommendation is explained.

## Final CTA

**Step Into Sagarvani --- Marine Intelligence for Everyone**

From open water to the coastline --- one validated answer, however you
reach us.
