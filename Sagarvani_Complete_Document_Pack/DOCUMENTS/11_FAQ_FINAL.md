# Sagarvani --- FAQ Copy

## What is Sagarvani?

Sagarvani is a conversational marine decision-intelligence platform that
combines ocean, weather, geospatial and risk information through ORCA's
collaborative agent architecture.

## What is ORCA?

ORCA is Sagarvani's orchestration and reasoning architecture. It
coordinates specialist marine agents, combines their outputs, checks
evidence for contradictions, and supports a validated, explainable
response.

## What data sources does Sagarvani use?

The project design references INCOIS, IMD, ISRO Bhuvan/NRSC, Bhoonidhi,
MOSDAC, BHASHINI and Sarvam AI, alongside supporting geospatial, weather
and technology infrastructure.

## Who is Sagarvani for?

The primary focus is fishermen/coastal crews, maritime operators,
researchers and disaster-management/coastal-safety users. The broader
stakeholder model also includes aquaculture, tourism, offshore energy
and policy/conservation.

## How does it work without a smartphone?

The product is designed around multiple access paths, including a
helpline and low-bandwidth communication portal. Full production IVR
integration is beyond the current web MVP.

## Does Sagarvani support voice?

Yes. Voice input is included in the MVP interface, with the architecture
prepared for BHASHINI/Sarvam-based multilingual speech services.

## How does Sagarvani handle conflicting data?

Conflicting or low-confidence outputs trigger a visible
re-check/re-planning state. The system should expose the validation
result rather than silently hiding uncertainty.

## Does Sagarvani replace official authorities?

No. It is a decision-support platform designed to synthesize evidence
and explain recommendations. Official warnings and authorities remain
important sources of operational truth.

## What problem statement is it built for?

Smart India Hackathon 2026, SIH26176 --- ORCA: Marine Ecosystem
Reasoning with Collaborative Agents, under Disaster Management /
Software.
