# Sagarvani --- Stakeholder & Impact Framework

## Priority stakeholders

### 1. Fishermen & Coastal Crews

Need: simple, location-aware, understandable marine guidance. Value:
combine weather, wave, ocean and risk context into one explainable
interaction.

### 2. Marine & Port Operators

Need: operational situational awareness and route/context information.
Value: spatial intelligence, alerts, environmental conditions and
evidence.

### 3. Ocean Researchers & Scientists

Need: integrated datasets and analytical context. Value: cross-source
exploration and explainable synthesis.

### 4. Disaster Management & Coastal Safety

Need: rapid situational awareness and warning context. Value:
consolidated environmental signals, spatial context and visible
validation.

### 5. Aquaculture & Marine Farming

Need: environmental condition awareness. Value: integrated ocean/weather
indicators and spatial context.

### 6. Coastal Tourism & Recreation

Need: understand marine and weather conditions. Value: accessible,
explainable marine context.

### 7. Offshore Energy / Marine Industry

Need: operational and environmental awareness. Value: spatial decision
support and multi-source evidence.

### 8. Marine Policy & Conservation

Need: cross-domain marine evidence. Value: integrated environmental
indicators and explainable synthesis.

## Impact framing

### Social

-   More accessible marine information
-   Local-language/voice pathway
-   Low-connectivity design consideration

### Economic

-   Better-informed trip and operational decisions
-   Reduced manual effort in interpreting fragmented information
-   Support for marine operations and research workflows

### Environmental

-   Better access to ocean-state and environmental indicators
-   Spatial understanding of marine conditions
-   Evidence-oriented decision support

Do not publish unsupported percentages, monetary savings, accuracy
claims or user counts.
