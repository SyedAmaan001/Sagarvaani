# Sagarvani --- Brand & Asset Guide

## Source of truth

The supplied `logo sagarvani.png` is the primary logo asset.

## Image direction

Use: - real marine/coastal environments - fishermen/coastal crews -
port/maritime operations - researchers - disaster/coastal safety
contexts - aquaculture - tourism - offshore energy - conservation -
satellite/ocean data visual language

Avoid: - generic corporate office stock - fake customer headshots - fake
testimonials - arbitrary tech imagery - text baked into generated
imagery

## Existing supplied image prompts

See `05_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPTS.md` in SOURCE_MATERIAL.

## Asset behavior

-   Hero: WaveHero + product overlay, not a generic dashboard
    screenshot.
-   Agent cards: use a coherent family of marine/scientific imagery.
-   Data source strip: prefer logos/wordmarks only where their use is
    appropriate.
-   Dashboard: prioritize data visualization over decorative imagery.

## Typography

The supplied Montroc pack can support display/headline treatments. Keep
dense UI legible and restrained.

## Color

Electric Tundra only. Cyan is reserved for live state.
