# Sagarvani — Marine Stakeholder Research

## Research objective

Identify real marine/ocean stakeholder groups and translate their needs into accurate Sagarvani value propositions.

This document is intended for the website's stakeholder section and impact messaging.

Do not present Sagarvani as a replacement for official safety warnings, navigation authorities, scientific expertise, or emergency command structures.

Sagarvani is decision intelligence and synthesis.

---

# 1. Fishermen & Coastal Crews

## Why this group matters

Indian fisheries depend heavily on marine conditions and timely information.

INCOIS provides ocean-state, potential-fishing-zone and small-vessel advisories, including forecasts and boat-specific safety information. INCOIS states that its short-term ocean-state forecasts serve fisherfolk as well as shipping, oil and gas, Navy and Coast Guard users.

## Sagarvani value

Sagarvani can act as a synthesis layer above multiple feeds.

Potential experiences:

- ask "Is it safe to leave at 5 AM?"
- combine wind, waves, currents and warnings
- provide location-aware explanations
- provide local-language outputs
- expose confidence/evidence
- support low-bandwidth/voice pathways

## Website wording

`Safer trip decisions from the conditions that actually matter.`

Avoid saying:
"guarantees safe fishing."

# 2. Marine & Port Operators

## Evidence

INCOIS describes sea-state data as important for vessel entry/exit, including small fishing vessels and large tankers. It also describes applications for port operations and offshore activity.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- route context
- sea-state synthesis
- current/wave/wind context
- port-approach conditions
- geospatial restrictions
- hazard overlays
- operational risk summaries

## Website wording

`Bring sea state, weather and spatial risk into one operational view.`

---

# 3. Ocean Researchers & Scientists

## Evidence

INCOIS states that ocean observational, satellite and related data are quality-checked, archived and made available to students, researchers and other users.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- combine heterogeneous marine datasets
- explore trends
- investigate spatial relationships
- compare environmental signals
- surface relevant evidence
- reduce context switching between sources

## Website wording

`Explore marine signals across sources without losing the evidence trail.`

Do not claim that Sagarvani replaces scientific analysis.

---

# 4. Disaster Management & Coastal Safety

## Evidence

The Indian Coast Guard describes coastal security coordination among agencies and community interaction with fishermen/coastal populations. INCOIS also publishes high-wave, cyclone, tsunami and other hazard advisories.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- combine hazard context
- summarize relevant warnings
- show affected areas spatially
- compare sources
- surface contradictions
- support rapid situational awareness
- provide explainable evidence

## Website wording

`Turn fragmented warning feeds into one explainable operational picture.`

Never claim that Sagarvani itself issues official alerts unless that becomes an approved system function.

---

# 5. Aquaculture & Marine Farming

## Evidence basis

Aquaculture depends on environmental conditions and marine/freshwater resource management. ICAR identifies fisheries and aquaculture research and technology development as major areas.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- localized weather/ocean conditions
- environmental trend awareness
- temperature and water-condition context
- risk monitoring
- multi-source alerts/context

## Website wording

`Connect farm decisions to the environmental signals around them.`

This should remain a decision-support proposition until actual farm-specific data products are implemented.

---

# 6. Coastal Tourism & Recreation

## Evidence

INCOIS explicitly notes relevance of sea-state information to recreational coastal tourism and sea-plane operations.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- sea-state awareness
- wind/wave context
- hazard awareness
- location-specific conditions
- operational planning

## Website wording

`Plan coastal activity around the conditions that matter.`

Avoid promising "perfect weather."

---

# 7. Offshore Energy & Marine Industry

## Evidence

INCOIS states that marine activities around India include high-tech industries such as oil exploration, offshore operations and transportation, and that sea-state information supports offshore operations.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- operational weather/sea-state context
- geospatial constraints
- hazard overlays
- route/operation context
- evidence-backed synthesis

## Website wording

`Give offshore decisions the full marine context.`

Do not overstate autonomous control.

---

# 8. Marine Policy, Conservation & Planning

## Evidence

Marine-management literature defines stakeholders broadly to include parties that influence or are affected by marine activities and decisions, including fishers, aquaculture, offshore extraction, renewable energy, tourism, transport and other groups.

Research also emphasizes the importance of integrating knowledge across disciplines and stakeholders for marine decision-support.

## Sagarvani value

Potential capabilities:

- multi-source evidence synthesis
- spatial context
- environmental indicators
- scenario exploration
- explainable outputs

## Website wording

`Make complex marine evidence easier to explore, compare and act on.`

---

# Recommended website priority

Lead with:

1. Fishermen & Coastal Crews
2. Marine & Port Operators
3. Ocean Researchers & Scientists
4. Disaster Management & Coastal Safety

Then show:

5. Aquaculture
6. Coastal Tourism
7. Offshore Energy / Marine Industry
8. Policy / Conservation

This keeps the most direct Sagarvani use cases first while demonstrating that the platform can serve the broader ocean ecosystem.
